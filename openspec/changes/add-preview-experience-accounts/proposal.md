## Why

The first round of free online web trial is for recruiters and overseas users to experience. Users have confirmed five independent experience accounts: one for the back-end platform administrator, one for safety officer, and three ordinary mobile accounts for one person to build a house and two people to join. Currently there is no experience with the account initialization command. The password login and SMTP startup configuration share the switch. These accounts cannot be provided according to normal password authentication when email is disabled.

## What Changes

- Separate password authentication and email processes; trial configuration allows password login, retains Google login, disables email registration, verification, resending, retrieval, reset and email binding, and does not allow verification-free self-service registration.
- Provides controlled, idempotent, and environment-bound five-account initialization, reuses password policies, hashes, sessions, and authorizations, and does not pre-issue tokens or pre-build rooms.
- Clarify the source of preset credentials, distinguish controlled initialization and real email verification; **BREAKING**: The login method projected `verifiedAt` allows null for experience credentials without email verification, and adds identifiable source information, updates unique OpenAPI and generates clients.
- The final administrative roles are only `PLATFORM_ADMIN` and only `SAFETY_OFFICER`; the three mobile accounts have no administrative roles, and the room host is generated by normal room creation. Reuse existing bootstrap and audit role commands, retaining last administrator protection.
- Three mobile accounts are pre-filled with complete adult information; when the email is closed, the UI does not display false successes that have been sent/to be received, and the username, password and Google login are retained.
- Retain temporary credentials and payload period cleanup when disabling delivery; provide credential distribution, target verification, initialization recovery, cleanup and rollback runbook, and authentication/authorization/three-person room acceptance plan.

### Confirmed Scope

Five independent accounts, separation of the above roles, no emails in the first round, retaining Google, and discussing the official launch after a web trial. The current authorization only includes planning; the database, remote account and deployment are not authorized for execution.

### Non-Goals

Email-free self-service registration and retrieval, no verification for all users, global HOST role, persistent demo room, administrator rights simulation, automatic account association/merger, supplier selection, purchase of server or domain name, remote deployment, real sending and acceptance, formal production online.

### Unresolved Decisions And Recommendations

- The target database, trial environment identifier and public network address have not been determined. They are pre-inputs for initialization/migration/public network acceptance and do not affect this planning.
- The actual usernames, secret inputs, and distribution channels for the five accounts have not yet been selected; the repository does not save the actual credentials.
- It is recommended that only high-privilege accounts be distributed to controlled experience users, and ordinary visitors use Google or ordinary experience accounts; this is a suggestion. The user has not yet confirmed the distribution target, and does not write it as a product MUST.
- The Figma Desktop Bridge is currently not connected; the certified V2 target is from the repository acceptance record, and the email deactivation status has not yet been verified by the real-time canvas. The Bridge needs to be restored and the original page status must be confirmed before UI implementation.

## Capabilities

- `preview-auth-controls`: Independently control password authentication, email process, Google portal, and interface, session, cleanup and source projection boundaries after email is closed in the trial environment.
- `preview-experience-accounts`: Five account controlled initialization, independent identity, complete information, role separation, idempotent conflict handling, secret input and cleanup.

### Modified Capabilities

No main specification modification. `backoffice-access-control`, `backoffice-audit` and room rules maintain the original requirements; the existing implementations of mailbox authentication, mobile mailbox pages and background workspaces come from active changes that have not yet been synchronized. The new trial specifications of this change clearly define environment exceptions and do not cover them silently. Conflicts need to be jointly handled during review/synchronization.
