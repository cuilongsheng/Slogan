## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Member avatars open the corresponding report form

Selecting another joined member's room avatar MUST open reporting directly with that member selected. The reporter's own avatar MUST not allow a self-report. Existing report categories, description bounds, retry identity, confirmation and dismissal behavior MUST remain.

#### Scenario: Avatar selection

- **WHEN** a participant selects another joined member's avatar
- **THEN** the report form identifies that member without first showing the entire member-management list
